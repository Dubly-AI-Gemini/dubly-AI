Dubly AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Dubly AI is a Chrome extension for live video dubbing. The extension does not operate a developer-owned backend for storing your audio, transcripts, translations, or API key.

API key

Your Google Gemini API key is stored in Chrome local extension storage. When you use dubbing or voice testing, the extension sends requests directly from the extension to the Google Gemini API using the key you supplied.

Audio and video

While dubbing is active, the extension captures audio from the tab you selected. Audio chunks are sent to Google Gemini for processing. The extension does not upload that audio to a separate Dubly server.

Settings

Language, theme, volume, font, voice, and related preferences are stored locally in Chrome.

Donations

The support page only displays the wallet address supplied by the publisher. The extension does not process or custody cryptocurrency payments.

Third parties

Google Gemini API is a third-party service. Its use is governed by Google's applicable terms, policies, and account configuration.

Contact

Publishers should add their preferred support/contact address before submitting this policy to the Chrome Web Store.
